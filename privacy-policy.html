Homewardly Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 24, 2026 · Developer: Stellar Core

Plain-English Summary: Homewardly is a 100% local-first family organizer. We do not operate cloud servers, we do not collect or sell your personal data, and we do not include ads, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs.

1. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Everything you enter in Homewardly—including family member names, chore lists, star balances, meal plans, shopping lists, calendar events, notes, home maintenance schedules, emergency/babysitter information, and your Parent PIN—is stored strictly on your device (in local app storage).

If you choose to enable Host Family Hub mode, your device runs a local Wi-Fi server inside your home network so other family devices on the same Wi-Fi can sync directly with your hub using an 8-character pairing code. None of this data passes through Stellar Core or any external server.

2. Network Requests Made by the App

Homewardly makes network requests only for the following optional features:

  • Local Weather Forecast (Open-Meteo): When displaying the weather tile or searching for your city, the app sends your chosen city name or latitude/longitude coordinates to Open-Meteo to retrieve the forecast. No personal identifiers, device IDs, or family data are sent.
  • Local Wi-Fi Sync (Optional): If you connect to a Host Family Hub on your home Wi-Fi, data is sent directly over your local network to the IP address you configure, authenticated by your hub's pairing code.

QR codes (both Wi-Fi QR codes on the Emergency One-Sheet and Phone Sync QR codes) and fonts are generated and bundled entirely on-device—never sent to external services.

3. Data Sharing, Ads & Analytics

  • We do not collect, transmit, rent, or sell any personal information.
  • Homewardly contains no third-party advertising networks, behavioral tracking, or crash/analytics telemetry.

4. Children's Privacy

Homewardly is designed to be set up and managed by parents and adult caregivers to organize household routines. Because all family data remains exclusively on the user's own devices and local network, Stellar Core never collects, stores, or has access to information about children.

5. Backups & Deleting Your Data

You control your data completely:

  • You can export or restore a local JSON backup file at any time from Home Ops.
  • You can permanently delete all app data by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling Homewardly.

6. Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will post the revised version at this URL and update the effective date above.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact Stellar Core via the developer email listed on the Homewardly Google Play Store listing.
